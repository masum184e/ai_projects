### Background

NSTU was created to provide quality science and technology education for the southern coastal region of Bangladesh. The university engages in teaching, research and societal service for rural and urban populations. Humanities are included to develop leaders who can "fashion a more humane and just world".

Highlights from the About page:

- About one-fourth of the teachers are pursuing PhDs in different parts of the world.
- It is the only university in Bangladesh teaching three exclusively information-based subjects: Information Science, Information Technology, and Information and Communication Engineering.
- International conferences, seminars and symposiums are held frequently.
- Internal programming contests are held regularly, and many national and international programming-contest awards have been won.
- Fisheries & Marine Science and Oceanography students contribute to the country's blue economy through research on marine resources.
- National and international days and cultural festivals are celebrated with local residents, making the campus a "cultural capital" of the southern region.
- The campus is modeled on western universities such as Cambridge, with a calm, green environment.

### Vision

- **Homepage:** To be a leading center of excellence in education, research, and innovation, fostering global competence, sustainability, and socio-economic development for a prosperous Bangladesh.
- **About page:** To become the center of excellence for advanced research and technology to achieve sustainable development.

### Mission

1. Create precious manpower with intellectual, creative, technical and practical skills to contribute with moral, industry, community and caring relationship among all stakeholders.
2. Provide outcome-based standard education as preparation for advanced studies, entrepreneurship or future employment across a global range of ICT, business, research organization along with social and public institutions.
3. Ensure the delivery of high-quality learning combining with hands-on training through qualified, dedicated and experienced facilitators.
4. Deliver research and innovative opportunities at all levels to promote local and global networking for students and staff.
5. Engage in partnerships with local and global communities, government agencies, industries and universities aspiring for sustainable society and economy.

## 4. Institute of Information Technology (IIT)

- **Director:** Mohammed Nizam Uddin
- **Tagline:** "Where Knowledge Meets Industry and Sustainable Grows"
- **Programs:** B.Sc. (Hons) in Software Engineering; M.Sc. in Software Engineering (MSSE); M.Sc. in Information Technology (MIT); and PGDIT (listed under IIT in the programs table).

### Vision
To create competent and skilled intellectual IT professionals specifically for planning strategically, implementing and managing IT systems along with empowerment, enhancement and engagement of the academic, support and research activities.

### Mission
IIT aims to produce competent IT professionals in Bangladesh with a strong understanding of information technology bodies of knowledge and theories, who can apply sound technological principles to the cost-effective creation, development, operation and maintenance of high-quality information systems, and who are prepared for lifelong learning. Specific goals:

- **M1.** Uplift the rural communities of Bangladesh, specifically the Noakhali region, through advanced quality education in IT.
- **M2.** Disseminate IT knowledge to the university community, professional societies, local communities, the state and the nation through advanced research and development.
- **M3.** Provide students with the tools to become productive, participating global citizens and life-long learners.
- **M4.** Maintain relationships with industry through effective and dynamic industry-academia collaboration.
- **M5.** Provide an atmosphere for students and faculty for continuous learning to investigate, apply and transfer knowledge.
- **M6.** Provide diversity and professionalism in the IT field along with an entrepreneurial, interdisciplinary, service-oriented environment and curriculum.

### Student organizations and activities
NSTU Model United Nations Association (NSTUMUNA), NSTU Debating Society, NSTU English Club, NSTU Business Club, NSTU Blood Donors Society, DreamBazz Filmz, Moshal, Protiddhoni, NSTU Photographic Society, NSTU Shine (volunteer organization), BNCC, Rover Scout. - **Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs):** supports the UN SDGs through education, research and community partnerships.
